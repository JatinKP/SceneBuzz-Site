Privacy policy

Effective: October 10, 2026

SceneBuzz is a slideshow app for Android. This policy explains what the app does with information on your device.

Your photos, videos and music

SceneBuzz reads the photos, videos and audio files you choose so it can show and play them. They stay on your device. SceneBuzz does not upload them to us or to anyone else.

Settings and logs

Your slide profiles, settings, and the optional proof-of-play log are stored only on your device. If you export the proof-of-play report, it is handed to an app you pick (for example your email app); SceneBuzz does not send it by itself.

Library Sync

If you turn on Library Sync, SceneBuzz copies profiles and media directly between your own devices on your local Wi-Fi network, encrypted with a pairing code. Nothing goes through the internet or any server we run.

Face-aware crop

SceneBuzz uses Google ML Kit face detection on the device to frame photos. The images are analyzed on your device and are not sent anywhere. Google's ML Kit library may send Google limited technical information (such as device model, OS version, app version, performance metrics and a per-installation identifier) for diagnostics and usage analytics, as described at https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/android-data-disclosure.

No accounts, no ads, no selling

SceneBuzz has no sign-in, shows no ads, and we do not sell or share personal information.

Children

SceneBuzz is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.
