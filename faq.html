Questions, answered.

Which devices does it run on?

Android devices, including old Kindle Fire tablets, Chromebooks and newer phones and tablets.

Where do photos come from?

Local folders on your device. Pick the Camera folder, or browse other folders.

Will my photos be cropped?

Not with “fit whole photo”. The edges get a dimmed or blurred version of the photo.

How long is each photo shown?

30 seconds by default, adjustable up to 180 seconds. Transitions are 2 seconds by default.

Can I run more than one setup?

Yes. Create profiles like “Living Room”.

When is it available?

SceneBuzz is in Google Play closed testing. The public release is coming soon.
